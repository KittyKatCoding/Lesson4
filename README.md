# Request for Proposal: Wireless Infrastructure Upgrade

## Organization: Tomato High School

## Location: Vegetable City, Manalberta, Geranica

## Organization Overview

Tomato High School is a private high school with 600 students in Vegetable City. Tomato High School was founded in 1967, with our first group of 30 students and five teachers. We are a medium-sized school that is for students that are strong in arts and sciences. Tomato High School has a very good musical theatre program that puts on two musical productions every year. Our musicals regularly get awards from the Manalberta Theatre Association. Our facilities also include aa state of the art Biological Laboratory featuring a freshwater aquarium with native fish, and a history museum in the school where students can come to learn any time.

Tomato High School strives in academic excellence, and has the fifth best scores in English, Math, and Science in the province of Manalberta

For 48 years, Tomato school has had a traditional method of teaching with limited technology. Our math program does not use calculators, and in English and Arts classes all assignments are to be written.

This year our board has decided that we should start implementing technology use, as we need to prepare our students for the real world where they need to use technology in the workforce.

This year we purchased new laptops for all our students as we are planning on completing most of our coursework on laptops instead of paper. In the past all student assignments were done on paper, with only teachers and staff needing the Wi-Fi. Now that all of the students have Wi-Fi, we need to improve our WiFi, as our current setup can not support 600 students plus 30 staff.

# Project Objectives

Tomato High has not updated its Wi-Fi since 2012, and it is very slow. On average the download speed is 1 Mbps, and it disconnects frequently. Tomato School needs a new wireless network with reliable coverage, strong security, modern performance, centralized management, and support for future growth. This wireless network is needed, because the current network is set up with only a few APs, and teachers and students can not complete their assignments on their laptops because half of the time the Wi-Fi works and is very slow, and the other half of the time there is no connection at all.

The objective of this project is to deploy a modern and future-proof wireless network that provides:

- Reliable Wi-Fi 6 , 6E or 7 coverage throughout the school
  
- Connectivity for 700+ devices
  
- Enhanced enterprise-grade security
  
- WPA3-Enterprise Security
  
- Individual logins for each staff or student member
  
- Separate SSID for Student and Staff Networks
  
- Student Network is on VLAN10, Staff Network is on VLAN 20
  
- Future-proof capacity for 1000 devices
  

### Scope of Work

Currently, we only have 2 APs in the hallway, and after doing a wireless survey we found that the Wi-Fi signal strength in all of the classrooms was only 5 to 20 percent.

We need to have one access point installed on the ceiling of each class room to provide reliable connection strength to all school laptops. We already have a restricted and monitored school network setup. The only configuration of the network that needs to be improved is to have new access points installed. The students and staff should be on separate SSIDs, and should log in with their assigned ID number and password. The students are on VLAN 10 and the staff is on VLAN 20. That is because students should not be able to print on a staff printer, and staff have less restriction to websites than students.

The wireless access points should either be secured with WPA3-Enterprise or WPA2-Enterprise, all school devices support WPA3.

We have 600 students and 30 staff, so we need to have support for at least 630 devices.

All of these requirements are mandatory.
